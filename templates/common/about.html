About

Budgetly keeps income, expenses, savings and investments for each month on their own.

Categories

  • Income is money coming in.
  • Expenses is money spent.
  • Savings is money set aside.
  • Investments is money put to work.

Totals

Net is income minus expenses, savings and investments.

The savings rate is savings plus investments, divided by income. When income is 0, the rate is shown as a dash.

Amounts

Every entry is a positive amount. The category decides whether the money came in or went out.
